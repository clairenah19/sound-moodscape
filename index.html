Free to explore, no setup needed. The map, mood scores, real photos, and the plain-language explanation of how each place becomes music all work instantly. Audio is the exception: playback needs either an MP3 in the audio/ folder or your own Suno API key — there is no built-in synthesiser, so without one of those the player has nothing to play. The "Ask a Local" chat likewise needs a Gemini key. This is a deliberate bring-your-own-key design for a static site with no backend server, not a bug or a paywall you'll hit unexpectedly.
Research status. Regional activity scores use an explicit composite of official density, tourism, noise, and visitor-survey data. The weights are hypotheses, not measured ISO Eventfulness. Landmark scores remain illustrative. Wikipedia popularity is shown separately for comparison. See About & research for methods and pending validation.
