Free to explore, no setup needed. The map, mood scores, real photos, and a synth soundscape all work instantly. Two optional AI features — the "Ask a Local" chat and AI-generated music — need your own free/paid API key (Gemini / Suno), entered when you try them. This is a deliberate bring-your-own-key design for a static site with no backend server, not a bug or a paywall you'll hit unexpectedly.
Research status. Each province's mood score is now computed live from Pleasantness/ Eventfulness soundscape research (ISO 12913) — sourced from a published study for 2 of 17 regions, reasoned estimates for the rest, shown with a Sourced/Estimated/Partial badge in each province's panel. Per-place scores within a province are still hand-tuned. See the About & research page for exactly what's built vs. proposed, including the still-unwired Density Score and Seasonal Drift.
