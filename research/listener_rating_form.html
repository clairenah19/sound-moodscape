Before you begin

This exploratory study takes about 12–18 minutes. Use headphones if available, choose a comfortable volume, and keep the same listening setup throughout.

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time or delete this browser's saved responses. We collect a random participant code, ratings, playback completion, presentation order, and timing. Do not enter your name or contact details.

Responses remain in this browser until you download a CSV. Nothing is automatically submitted. Share the downloaded file with the researcher only if you choose to participate. Your anonymous file will be used to study how people interpret these clips.

Listen to the entire clip at least once before continuing. Replays are allowed.

To what extent do you agree that the atmosphere communicated by this music is each of the following?

1 = strongly disagree · 2 = disagree · 3 = neither · 4 = agree · 5 = strongly agree.
